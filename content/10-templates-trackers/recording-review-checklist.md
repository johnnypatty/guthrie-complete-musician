## First listen — music only

- [ ] Pulse/form remains stable
- [ ] Phrases breathe
- [ ] Motifs return/develop
- [ ] Harmony changes are acknowledged
- [ ] Dynamics create direction
- [ ] Ending feels intentional

## Second listen — craft

- [ ] Attacks and releases are clean
- [ ] Unused strings are controlled
- [ ] Bends reach chosen targets
- [ ] Vibrato onset/rate/width are deliberate
- [ ] Fast passages retain subdivision
- [ ] Gain/ambience do not hide detail

## Third pass — communication

- [ ] Body looks reasonably relaxed
- [ ] Spoken introduction is clear and concise
- [ ] Mistakes are recovered from without collapse
- [ ] Performance has one memorable identity
