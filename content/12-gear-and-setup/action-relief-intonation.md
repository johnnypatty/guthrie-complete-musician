## Use the right order

Confirm strings and tuning first, then neck relief, action, intonation, and pickup height. Measure the original state before changing it.

- Too little relief can buzz in lower positions; too much can feel high in the middle.
- Action is personal, but every fret should speak at your real picking force and wide bends should not choke.
- Compare the fretted 12th-fret note with the harmonic using normal pressure. Sharp means lengthen the string; flat means shorten it.
- Retune before every reading. On a floating bridge, adjustments interact.

Stop if a truss rod feels stuck, a saddle screw is damaged, or the setup needs fret work rather than ordinary adjustment.
