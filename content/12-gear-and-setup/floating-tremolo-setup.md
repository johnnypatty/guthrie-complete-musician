## The bridge is a balance

A floating tremolo settles where string tension equals spring tension. Change tuning or string gauge and the bridge angle changes until the balance is restored.

## Safe sequence

1. Photograph the starting bridge angle and measure action.
2. Replace with the intended gauge, stretch gently, and tune repeatedly.
3. If the bridge tilts, make small equal claw-screw changes with the guitar supported and the locking nut open.
4. Retune after every adjustment; bridge angle and pitch affect each other.
5. Check relief, action, intonation, pickup clearance, return to zero, then lock and use fine tuners near midrange.

Do not file a nut, level frets, or force worn studs without the right tools and experience. A technician is cheaper than damaged knife edges or anchors.
