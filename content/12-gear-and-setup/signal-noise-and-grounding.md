## Identify the path

If noise disappears when a laptop charger is unplugged but the audio device stays connected, a USB/power ground loop is likely. Test on battery, move the charger and display away, use one power strip, and disconnect nonessential USB devices one at a time.

Do not remove protective earth or use unsafe ground-lifting mains adapters. For balanced monitor paths, a proper audio isolator can break the signal loop while safety earth remains intact.

Record a clean DI or restrained preset, set input below clipping, and use the lowest gate that closes during real rests. Fix power and routing before hiding noise with a threshold that cuts sustain.
