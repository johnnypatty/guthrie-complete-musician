## Gauge changes are setup changes

Moving from 9–42 to 10–46 increases tension. A fixed bridge may need relief and intonation checks; a floating tremolo also needs spring balance restored.

Breakage in the middle of a string can come from fatigue, corrosion, aggressive bending, a very hard pick attack, or a damaged contact point. Breakage at the same bridge or nut location suggests a burr that needs inspection.

Change one variable at a time, use a complete fresh set, stretch and tune patiently, and record the gauge. You can learn routine changes and balance, but permanent nut work belongs to a good technician.
