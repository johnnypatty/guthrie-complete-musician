Create one 12–20 minute recorded set. It may be filmed in separate continuous pieces, but do not edit individual notes.

## Required program

1. **Famous melody arrangement:** a well-known short melody reharmonized or arranged in your own way.
2. **Expressive cover:** a Gary Moore, Jeff Beck, Andy Timmons, Pink Floyd, or similar piece/section learned principally by ear.
3. **Technical project:** one demanding section from your personal ladder, played at the fastest tempo that remains musical.
4. **Improvised solo:** 2–3 minutes over changing harmony, with audible motifs, space, dynamics, chord targets, and a planned ending.
5. **Original statement:** a 60–120 second composition or etude combining at least three skills from the course.
6. **Communication:** concise spoken introductions that sound like you.

## Evaluation

Score 0–5 with written evidence:

- time/groove;
- pitch/bends/vibrato;
- articulation/muting;
- technique serving the phrase;
- harmonic awareness;
- motif and development;
- dynamics and form;
- recovery from mistakes;
- original decisions;
- audience communication.

## Final comparison

Watch the baseline and final set on different days. Write:

- five objective improvements;
- three remaining bottlenecks;
- two sounds/styles worth exploring next;
- one six-month project that genuinely excites you.

The course succeeds when you can diagnose and direct your own learning more intelligently—not when the work is “finished.”
