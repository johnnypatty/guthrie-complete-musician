Run at the end of weeks 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, and 24. Use the same recording setup and compare to your baseline, not to a polished artist recording.

## Every month

1. **Cold technique:** two clean takes of the current picking and legato tests.
2. **Pulse:** one minute with a sparse click and one minute of funk/odd-meter work.
3. **Bend/vibrato:** target pitch plus controlled width/rate examples.
4. **Ear:** map a new 10–20 second fragment under a fixed time limit.
5. **Harmony:** spell/build random chords and connect one progression in three neck areas.
6. **Improvisation:** free, restricted-note, and chord-target takes over the same progression.
7. **Performance:** one uncut repertoire take with a short introduction.

## Phase-specific test

### Week 4

Name natural notes quickly, build major/minor/diminished triads, improvise a clear motif with rests, and finish one Level-1 melody by ear.

### Week 8

Build common seventh chords, demonstrate deliberate bend/vibrato choices, connect two scale areas without running a shape, and transcribe a short expressive phrase.

### Week 12

Voice-lead a progression with triad inversions, hold a funk groove, target thirds/sevenths, and finish the first self-made cover.

### Week 16

Demonstrate Dorian/Lydian/Mixolydian as distinct sounds, phrase through an odd grouping, and transform one Guthrie-derived principle into an original line.

### Week 20

Apply sweep/tap/string-skip mechanics inside music, resolve one outside idea intentionally, and complete a demanding project section.

### Week 24

Complete the final performance specification.

## Comparison rule

A higher BPM with worse timing, muting, pitch, or tension is not improvement. Keep recordings whose musical control increased even when the number did not.
