## Purpose

Own the space between clicks so the metronome confirms your pulse rather than creating it.

## Method

### Subdivision ladder

At one tempo, clap/count and then play quarter notes, eighths, triplets, sixteenths, and back down. The click stays unchanged. Accent beat one and keep the final subdivision from rushing.

### Click placement ladder

1. Click on every quarter.
2. Click on beats 2 and 4.
3. Click once per bar on beat 1.
4. Click once per bar on another beat.
5. Click every two bars.

Do not advance until you can re-enter after a rest without guessing.

### Rhythm-only solo

Use one muted note and improvise for 60 seconds using rests, ties, accents, triplets, and sixteenths. Then use the same rhythm with a small pitch set.

### Internal count

Speak the smallest relevant subdivision quietly. Gradually stop speaking but keep hearing it. When timing collapses, restore the spoken grid.

## Success test

Playback remains centered for one minute, you can stop for one bar and re-enter, and sparse clicks do not reveal accumulated drift.

## Common mistake

Increasing BPM before subdivisions are even. Slow tempo exposes placement more honestly than fast tempo.
