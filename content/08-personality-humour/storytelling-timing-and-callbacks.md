## Build a story bank

Write ten true, small moments:

- first musical obsession;
- a practice disaster;
- a string breaking at the worst time;
- a misunderstood song title;
- buying or testing gear;
- an unexpected reaction from family/friend;
- a wrong note that became an idea;
- travel to a rehearsal/gig/shop;
- the contrast between how a passage sounds and feels to play;
- a goal that became stranger after learning more.

For each: situation, specific detail, change/surprise, what you realized. Truthful specificity is funnier than a generic joke.

## Compress

Tell one story in 90 seconds, then 45, then 20. Keep the detail that creates the image and remove setup that does no work.

## Musical callback

Callbacks can be played. Quote a tiny motif from the opening later in the solo, return to an earlier tone, or make the ending answer the introduction. Explain one callback in speech and perform another without explanation.

## Timing drill

Record the same line with no pause, short pause, and long pause before the reveal. Review which version feels conversational rather than theatrical. Apply the same exercise to a musical resolution.

## Weekly score

Rate 0–5: clarity, warmth, specificity, timing, listening, authenticity. Do not score only laughter; some good stories create attention or connection instead.

## Success test

You can introduce a song naturally, notice something happening in the room, and connect it later without derailing the music.
