Assessments • Weeks 1-24

Weekly Review

Complete this on the light day in 15–25 minutes.

Progressive assessments • weekly • review

Complete this on the light day in 15–25 minutes.

Evidence

  • Total focused minutes and number of days practised
  • Best clean tempo and the exact passage used
  • One rhythm recording
  • One transcription fragment
  • One constrained improvisation
  • Repertoire section completed
  • Any tension/pain warning

Five questions

  1. What became easier without forcing it?
  2. What mistake appeared in more than one context?
  3. Which idea sounded most like music rather than practice?
  4. What did playback reveal that playing did not?
  5. What single change will make next week better?

Score behavior, not identity

Rate 0–5: consistency, attention, timing, ear use, relaxation, musical risk, and honest review. Do not rate whether you are “talented.”

Choose next week

  • Keep one unresolved primary technique.
  • Choose one rhythm variable.
  • Choose one ear fragment.
  • Choose one harmony concept.
  • Choose one improvisation constraint.
  • Choose one repertoire deliverable.

If the same goal failed for two weeks, make it smaller or change the method rather than adding more hours.
