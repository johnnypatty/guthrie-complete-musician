Used Guitar Inspection

A model-neutral inspection route for neck, frets, hardware, electronics, and playability.

Reference gear • inspection • used-guitar

Bring a familiar reference

Use a tuner, known cable, small clean headphone or amp setup, pick, and phone light. Ask before adjusting anything.

Inspect in this order

  • Sight the neck for twist, cracks, repair lines, and extreme relief.
  • Play every fret on every string; test high bends for choking.
  • Look for fret grooves, lifting ends, stripped screws, leaning tremolo posts, and corrosion.
  • Test every switch position, the full knob travel, and the output jack while gently moving the cable.
  • Tune, use the bridge normally, and verify consistent return to pitch.

Walk away from undisclosed structural repair, a non-working truss rod, severe fret or neck trouble, damaged anchors, intermittent electronics, or pressure to skip inspection.
