Build Your Own Vocabulary

Vocabulary should become grammar you can recombine, not quotations pasted into unrelated conversations.

Intermediate to advanced improvisation-phrasing • build • vocabulary

Vocabulary should become grammar you can recombine, not quotations pasted into unrelated conversations.

The phrase card

For every borrowed phrase, record:

  • source and exact time range;
  • chord/progression and tonal center;
  • rhythm before pitches;
  • scale degrees/chord relationships;
  • contour and register;
  • articulation/dynamics;
  • emotional/function label;
  • three transformed versions;
  • one situation where it does not fit.

Transformation ladder

  1. reproduce accurately by ear;
  2. keep pitches, alter rhythm;
  3. keep rhythm, alter pitches;
  4. transpose to three keys;
  5. adapt major ↔ minor or static vamp ↔ moving changes;
  6. change articulation/register;
  7. combine only its principle with another influence;
  8. improvise until the original source is no longer obvious.

Cross-instrument month

Each month collect one idea from guitar, voice, bass, keyboard/horn/orchestra, and rhythm/drums. Examples: a vocal breath, bass anticipation, horn enclosure, piano voice leading, snare displacement.

Guthrie balance

Study Guthrie's rhythm, hearing, articulation, harmonic logic, and willingness to be playful. Do not measure success by how accurately strangers mistake you for him.

Success test

Use a studied principle spontaneously in three different improvisations without planning the exact notes, and explain what transferred.

Common mistake

Collecting hundreds of lick videos while none appear naturally in your own playing.
