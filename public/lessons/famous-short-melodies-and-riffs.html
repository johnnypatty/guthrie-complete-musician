Progressive ear-training • famous • short • melodies • riffs

These are short cultural “sentences.” Learn only the recognizable fragment first, in any comfortable key, with no tab. Then find it in two more neck areas and create one variation. Song/theme names are study targets; notation and lyrics are intentionally absent.

The rule

Do not race through the list. A fully heard five-second melody is worth more than ten half-guessed riffs. Choose one target per week alongside your personal repertoire project.
