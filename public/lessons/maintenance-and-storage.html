Maintenance and Storage

Keep the instrument stable with small regular checks rather than unnecessary modification.

Beginner gear • maintenance • storage

After playing

Wipe strings, hardware, neck back, and contact areas with a clean dry cloth. Store the guitar securely away from radiators, direct sun, damp, and fast temperature changes.

Monthly check

  • Listen for new crackle or intermittent output.
  • Check loose strap hardware, jack nut, and tremolo arm fit without overtightening.
  • Look for corrosion, fret wear, bridge-angle change, and case moisture.
  • Replace strings when tuning, intonation, feel, or tone degrades.

Service frets because measurable wear affects playing, not because a calendar says so. A good original fret job is better than unnecessary modification.
