Reference rg8570-buying-guide • person • inspection • checklist

Bring: clip-on tuner, familiar cable, small clean/gained headphone/amp setup if practical (GP-200 is fine), flashlight/phone light, pick, strap if allowed, and this checklist. Do not adjust the seller's guitar without permission.

Before travelling

  • Clear photograph of back-of-headstock serial received
  • Photograph of j.custom certificate/COA received
  • Exact address/time and pickup price (€2,000) reconfirmed
  • Original case, trem arm and accessories reconfirmed
  • Ask whether strings/tuning/setup are still factory gauge/E standard
  • Do not send a deposit or private banking data merely to “hold” it

Identity and completeness

  • Serial format and likely production year plausible
  • Model/finish/features match RG8570-BRE
  • Certificate/papers and case present; inspect rather than assume
  • Tremolo arm, multitool/keys and strap-lock parts present where applicable
  • Pickup markings/layout and controls appear original

Visual inspection under strong light

  • Headstock tips/edges: no cracks, repairs or severe impact
  • Locking nut and screws: no stripped heads, cracks or movement
  • Neck/body joint and behind nut: no suspicious finish line or repair
  • Body front/back/edges: document dents, chips, buckle rash
  • Tremolo stud areas: no cracks, leaning posts or damaged anchors
  • Hardware: corrosion/wear consistent with “two months at home” claim
  • Case condition, smell/moisture and latch function checked

Neck, frets and playability

  • Sight neck from headstock and bridge; no obvious twist
  • Relief looks sensible, not extreme forward/back bow
  • Play every fret on every string cleanly at practical pressure
  • Check high-register bends for choking
  • Inspect frets under angled light for grooves/flat spots/lifting ends
  • Run fingers gently along edges; j.custom treatment should feel smooth
  • Action feels usable; very high action may hide fret problems
  • Ask whether truss rod has been adjusted and whether it works normally

Lo-Pro Edge tremolo

  • Baseplate sits reasonably parallel for the current setup
  • Tune carefully, lock nut as intended, use fine tuners near midrange
  • Check tuning before movement
  • Perform gentle dips and pull-ups, then more normal use
  • Tuning returns consistently, not merely after manually nudging the bridge
  • Arm holder has controllable tension without severe wobble
  • Fine tuners turn smoothly; saddle/locking screws are not stripped
  • Ask directly about knife-edge/post damage or replacements
  • Check flutter/feel without abusing the instrument

If return-to-zero is poor, rule out old strings/setup before assuming destroyed knife edges—but price/risk changes until a specialist diagnoses it.

Electronics

  • Test all five switch positions at clean volume
  • Neck humbucker: full output, no intermittent signal
  • Intermediate positions: expected brighter/lower-output character
  • Middle pickup: clear response
  • Bridge humbucker: full output, no harsh intermittent crackle
  • Volume and tone sweep smoothly without cutting out
  • Output jack stays solid when cable is moved gently
  • Tap pickups lightly only with seller permission; no obvious microphonic squeal at normal gain

Musical test — 30 to 45 minutes

  • Clean chords across neck remain in tune enough to judge
  • Funk/muted playing feels responsive in positions 2/4
  • Singing bends/vibrato feel controllable
  • Fast alternate picking/legato do not fight neck/fret setup
  • Tremolo use returns to the song's pitch center
  • Seated and standing balance/comfort tested
  • Volume compared fairly, not confused with better tone
  • You still like it after the initial excitement

Payment and handover

  • Agreed price and everything included restated before payment
  • Written receipt includes date, model, serial, price, seller/buyer names/signatures and “used private sale” terms
  • Photograph/retain listing and conversation
  • Count/confirm payment safely; do not expose unnecessary personal/bank details
  • Guitar secured in its case before leaving

Walk-away reasons

Undisclosed repair/crack, questionable serial/papers, nonfunctional truss rod, severe fret/neck problem, damaged tremolo posts/knife edges, persistent tuning failure, intermittent electronics, missing promised items, pressure to skip inspection, or a feel you genuinely dislike.
