Arrangement and Performance Checklist

Choose at least one:

Progressive repertoire-covers • arrangement • performance • checklist

Before arranging

  • Exact reference recording/version chosen
  • Form and timestamps written by ear
  • Tonal center and likely chord/root map recorded
  • Melody/riff can be sung without playback
  • Difficult sections separated from complete-form practice

Make it yours

Choose at least one:

  • new intro or ending
  • different register/octave
  • chord-melody or harmony layer
  • rhythmic displacement or groove change
  • clean/dirty contrast
  • improvised section with defined boundaries
  • dynamics/form changed intentionally

Technical readiness

  • cold start succeeds twice
  • transitions work in isolation and context
  • performance tempo is below the tension threshold
  • bends/vibrato have target choices
  • unused strings remain controlled
  • tone supports articulation instead of hiding it

Performance

  • concise introduction prepared as ideas, not memorized syllables
  • count-in/pulse internalized
  • no restarting after small errors
  • visual/body tension checked
  • ending is intentional

Review

Watch once without sound for body/stage communication. Listen once without watching for music. Then review normally. Write one win, one audience-facing issue, and one precise next action.
